For years, Casa Villa has been treating Stamford guests to authentic Mexican flavors on East Main Street.

A Taste of Mexico

South of the Rio Grande, right in Stamford

Casa Villa was built around generous hospitality and recipes that taste like home — robust salsas, sizzling platters, and the relaxed rhythm of a true Mexican table.

Our team works to deliver the perfect dining experience every visit: warm service, memorable plates, and an atmosphere that invites you to stay awhile. Join us at 866 East Main Street for dine-in and pickup, and taste the same commitment to authentic flavor.

From weekday lunches to weekend gatherings and full-service catering, Casa Villa remains a Stamford destination for Mexican cuisine done with care.
