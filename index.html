Our Story

Where Mexico Comes
to Your Table

866 East Main St

Casa Villa Restaurant brings the exotic, robust flavours of authentic Mexican cuisine to the heart of Stamford, Connecticut. Every dish on our menu is crafted from time-honoured recipes — the kind passed down through generations south of the Rio Grande — prepared with fresh ingredients and served with genuine warmth.

"Exotic, robust flavors of authentic Mexican cuisine in a warm atmosphere inspired by south of the Rio Grande."

From sizzling fajitas and hand-rolled tacos al pastor to our silky flan and churros con dulce de leche, every plate tells a story of tradition, craft, and hospitality. Whether you join us for a leisurely dine-in, order for pickup or delivery, or let us cater your next event, you'll find the same care and flavour in every bite.
